1.1 Context Diagram

--- [P#170] ---
The Online Movie Ticket Booking System is a web-based software system designed to replace the traditional walk-in and phone-based processes for purchasing movie tickets, managing cinema operations, and tracking revenue at a single-branch cinema. The system enables customers to browse movies, select showtimes and seats, make online payments, and receive electronic tickets with QR codes — all through a responsive web interface.

1.3 User Requirements

--- [P#200] ---
1.3.1 Actors

--- [T#201] ---
# | Actor | Description
1 | Manager | Actors who have the highest rights in the system. The manager can manage all core modules including movies, genres, cinema rooms, schedules, food items, promotions, and employee accounts.
2 | Employee | Staff members who operate directly in the system. They can manage offline ticket selling, validate bookings (check-in via QR codes), and process food and beverage orders at the counter.
3 | Customer | Registered members of the system. In addition to guest features, customers can book tickets online, rate and review movies, manage their booking history, and update their personal profiles.
4 | Guest | Unregistered users who can operate the basic public functions of the system: view movie lists, view movie details, view promotions, check ticket prices and showtimes, and register for a new account.
5 | System Admin | Actors responsible for high-level system administration and security. They manage all user accounts (including viewing the account list, blocking/unblocking, and assigning roles), configure system settings (such as banners, policies) and view System Logs to ensure integrity and compliance.

7 | Payment | VNPay/MoMo Payment Callback API | Receives asynchronous payment status callbacks (IPN/webhook) from external payment gateways. Verifies payment signature, updates booking status to "Confirmed" on success or "Failed" on failure.
8 | Payment | Payment Timeout Handler | Monitors pending payment transactions. If a payment is not completed within 5 minutes of gateway redirect, the system automatically cancels the booking attempt and releases the reserved seats.
9 | Notification | Booking Confirmation Email Service | Sends a confirmation email with e-ticket QR codes, booking details, and invoice to the customer within 1 minute of successful payment.

13 | Statistics | Revenue Data Aggregation Job | A scheduled job (runs every 5 minutes) that aggregates ticket sales, food sales, and occupancy data for the revenue statistics dashboard.

16 | Ticket | Ticket Status Auto-Update Service | A scheduled job (runs every 30 minutes) that automatically updates ticket status from "Confirmed" to "Expired" when the showtime has passed and the ticket was not checked in.

--- [P#835] ---

--- [P#838] ---

--- [P#839] ---

--- [P#840] ---
Ticket printer at the counter for manual ticket creation (UC-48).

--- [P#841] ---
Standard input devices (keyboard, mouse, touchscreen) for all user interactions.

--- [P#843] ---

--- [P#844] ---
Payment Gateway: VNPay, MoMo, or ZaloPay API for online payment processing (UC-27).

--- [P#845] ---
Email Service (SMTP): For account registration confirmation, password reset, and booking confirmation emails.

--- [P#846] ---
Database: Microsoft SQL Server for data storage and retrieval.

--- [P#847] ---
Web Server: Apache Tomcat running Java Servlet/JSP application.

--- [P#848] ---
3.1.4 Communication Interfaces

--- [P#849] ---
The system shall communicate over HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher) for all client-server interactions. RESTful API endpoints shall be used for payment gateway integration. Email communication shall use SMTP over port 587 with TLS encryption.

--- [P#851] ---
3.2 Quality Attributes

--- [P#852] ---
3.2.1 Usability

--- [P#853] ---
USR-01: A new customer shall be able to complete the full ticket booking flow (select movie → select showtime → select seat → pay) within 5 minutes without prior training.

--- [P#854] ---
USR-02: The system shall display clear error messages in Vietnamese when the user provides invalid input, indicating the exact field and expected format.

--- [P#855] ---
USR-03: Navigation between major functions shall require no more than 3 clicks from the home page.

--- [P#856] ---
USR-04: The seat selection map shall use intuitive color coding: available (green), occupied (red), selected (blue), VIP (gold border).

--- [P#857] ---
USR-05: All management pages (Manager/Employee dashboards) shall include search, filter, and sort capabilities on list views.

--- [P#858] ---
USR-06: The system shall be responsive and usable on screen widths from 375px (mobile) to 1920px (desktop).

--- [P#859] ---
3.2.2 Performance

--- [P#860] ---
PER-01: Page load time shall not exceed 3 seconds under normal conditions (≤ 100 concurrent users).

--- [P#861] ---
PER-02: The seat availability map for a showtime shall load within 2 seconds, including real-time occupancy status.

--- [P#862] ---
PER-03: Ticket lookup and check-in verification (UC-46) shall return the result within 1 second to avoid customer queues.

--- [P#863] ---
PER-04: Payment processing (UC-27) shall complete within 15 seconds, including gateway response time. If timeout occurs, the system shall automatically mark the invoice as "Failed."

--- [P#864] ---
PER-05: Revenue statistics reports (UC-49) shall be generated within 5 seconds for a date range of up to 1 year.

--- [P#865] ---
PER-06: The system shall support at least 200 concurrent users during peak hours (e.g., weekend evenings, movie premiere events).

--- [P#866] ---
3.2.3 Security

--- [P#867] ---
SEC-01: All user passwords shall be stored using a one-way hashing algorithm (bcrypt or SHA-256 with salt). Plain-text passwords shall never be stored or logged.

--- [P#868] ---
SEC-02: The system shall enforce role-based access control (RBAC) with 5 roles: Guest, Customer, Employee, Manager, and System Admin. Each API endpoint and page shall verify the user's role before granting access.

--- [P#869] ---
SEC-03: User sessions shall expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. The system shall force re-authentication after session timeout.

--- [P#870] ---
SEC-04: The system shall prevent common web vulnerabilities including SQL Injection, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), and Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).

--- [P#871] ---
SEC-05: All sensitive actions (account deletion, role assignment, system configuration changes) shall be recorded in the SystemLog table with the actor's AccountID, action type, IP address, and timestamp.

--- [P#872] ---
SEC-06: Failed login attempts shall be limited to 5 within a 15-minute window. After exceeding this limit, the account shall be temporarily locked for 30 minutes.

--- [P#873] ---
3.2.4 Reliability & Availability

--- [P#874] ---
REL-01: The system shall have a minimum uptime of 99% during operating hours (8:00 AM – 12:00 AM daily).

--- [P#875] ---
REL-02: In case of a payment gateway failure, the system shall allow the customer to retry payment or switch to a different payment method without losing the selected seats for up to 10 minutes (seat hold mechanism).

--- [P#876] ---
REL-03: The database shall be backed up daily. ⚠ Not implemented in the current codebase: there is no backup/restore action anywhere in AdminServlet or elsewhere; this remains a planned requirement.

--- [P#877] ---
REL-04: The system shall gracefully handle unexpected errors by displaying a user-friendly error page instead of exposing stack traces or system internals.

3.2.5 Scalability

--- [P#879] ---
SCA-01: The database schema shall support adding new room types (e.g., ScreenX, Dolby Atmos) without modifying existing table structures (currently enforced via CHECK constraint on Room.RoomType).

--- [P#880] ---
SCA-02: The system shall support adding new payment methods (e.g., Apple Pay, bank transfer) by extending the PaymentMethod field without requiring database schema migration.

--- [P#881] ---
SCA-03: The system architecture shall allow horizontal scaling of the web server behind a load balancer for future growth.

--- [P#882] ---
3.2.6 Maintainability

--- [P#883] ---
MAI-01: The application shall follow the MVC (Model-View-Controller) architecture pattern to separate concerns between data access, business logic, and presentation.

--- [P#884] ---
MAI-02: All system configuration values (banner images, policy text, theme settings) shall be stored in the database and manageable through the admin UI (UC-50) without requiring code deployment.

--- [P#885] ---
MAI-03: The system shall maintain comprehensive audit logs in the SystemLog table for all critical operations, supporting troubleshooting and compliance auditing.

5.3 Other Requirements

--- [P#899] ---
5.3.1 Legal and Compliance

--- [P#900] ---
The system shall comply with Vietnamese regulations on personal data protection (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP). Customer personal information (name, phone number, email) shall be collected only for booking purposes and shall not be shared with third parties without explicit consent.

--- [P#901] ---
5.3.2 Localization

--- [P#902] ---
All user-facing content shall be displayed in Vietnamese by default, including system messages, form labels, and error notifications. Currency values shall be formatted in Vietnamese Dong (VND) with thousand separators (e.g., 90.000 VND).

--- [P#903] ---
5.3.3 Browser Compatibility

--- [P#904] ---
The system shall support the latest two major versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari. Internet Explorer is not supported.

--- [P#905] ---
5.3.4 Data Retention

--- [P#906] ---
Transaction records (invoices, tickets) shall be retained for at least 2 years for auditing and reporting purposes. System logs shall be retained for at least 1 year.
